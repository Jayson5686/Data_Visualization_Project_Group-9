About this dashboard

Turning a decade of records into a guided story

CrashMetrics brings together national trends, road-user and age-group comparisons, state and territory patterns, and First Nations hospitalisation data. Use the interactive views to explore changes and compare measures; each storyline also includes a data table.

How to explore the story

  1. 01 Start with the national trendSee how admissions and bed days changed across the years.
  2. 02 Compare groups and placesUse the linked controls to explore road users, ages, and jurisdictions.
  3. 03 Read values in contextCheck the notes and data tables, including explanations for suppressed values.
